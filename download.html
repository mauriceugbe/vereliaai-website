Install Verelia

iPhone, via TestFlight. iOS 16 or later.

  1. Install TestFlight, then tap the button above TestFlight is Apple's app for pre-release software. It's free, and it's how updates reach you while Verelia is in beta.

  3. Enter your business name and city The first scan takes about twenty seconds. You'll get your number, your band, and your rank against the businesses you're competing with.

Signed in but still seeing the access screen? Verelia checks your subscription by email address. If you paid with a different one, email support@vereliaai.com and we'll move it across.
