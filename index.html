    FAQ

    What business owners want to know.

    Do I need technical experience to use Verelia?

    No. We handle the connections, the setup and the upkeep. You get a performance view designed around how you run your business.

    What’s the difference between Business Performance and the Pulse Score?

    Business Performance measures what happens inside the business, using your own data. Pulse measures how your business looks from the outside: how customers find you and how you compare with nearby competitors. Pulse is $99 a month on its own and included with every Business Performance plan.

    What do I need to send you?

    Whatever you already use to run the business: a strategic plan, a scorecard, a spreadsheet, or a list of the numbers you check each week. Add your logo and the tools you use. We do the rest.

    Is the first month refundable?

    No. The first month pays for your wireframe and up to two rounds of changes, and that work happens during it. If the wireframe isn't right, cancel before approving it and you won't pay a setup fee.

    What does the setup fee cover?

    Defining your metrics, connecting your tools and pulling their history, reconciling the numbers against your records, building the approved design with your real data, testing every visual and filter, and setting up logins for your team. It is due once, when you approve your wireframe. You can pay it in full or as 3 monthly payments ($1,000 on Starter, $2,000 on Growth), which end automatically after the third. If you are already on Pulse, your first 3 months of Pulse payments, up to $297, come off it.

    What counts as a change request?

    Adding something new: a metric, a visual, a filter, a page section, a new tool, or a different calculation. Fixing anything that is wrong or broken, updating a target, and keeping the data refreshed are included and never count.

    Who owns my data?

    You do. We use it only to run your Business Performance view and Pulse Score. If you leave, we can export your data and then delete it. See Data security.

    Where is my data stored and who can see it?

    In encrypted, access-controlled cloud databases in the United States. Only you, the people you invite, and the Verelia staff working on your account can see it. Details are on our Data security page.

    Do I need a data warehouse or a data team?

    No. We host your data for you. Enterprise customers can have a dedicated Snowflake warehouse hosted by Verelia, or connect their own.

    What if my tool isn't on your list?

    Tell us when you sign up. If it has a way to export or share its data, we can usually connect it. We confirm every connection before you approve the wireframe.

    Can I cancel?

    Yes. Plans are month to month. The setup fee is not refunded once the build has started.
