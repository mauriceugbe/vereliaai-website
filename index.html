Our approach

Good decisions start with numbers you trust.

Most businesses already have more than enough data. The problem is knowing which numbers matter, whether they're accurate, and what they're telling you.

We start with your goals, define how success is measured, and bring the relevant data together. Every metric has a clear definition, every target has context, and every dashboard is built around the decisions you need to make. The result is more than a report. It's a reliable way to understand how your business is doing.

What goes into your setup

The setup fee pays for building your dashboard, not for a template.

  • Defining your metrics. Agreeing how each number is calculated and what it's measured against.
  • Connecting your systems. Authorizing read access to each tool and pulling its history.
  • Reconciling the figures. Checking our numbers against your own records until they match.
  • Building your pages. Turning the approved wireframe into a live dashboard with your data.
  • Testing accuracy. Checking every visual, filter and calculation before you go live.
  • Setting up access. Logins for you and your team, and a dashboard you keep using every month.

What counts as a change request

Your plan includes a set number of changes each month. Fixes never count against it.

Change request
  • Adding a new metric or visual
  • Adding a page section or a new filter
  • Changing how a metric is calculated
  • Connecting a tool you didn't have before
Included, never counted
  • Fixing a broken connection
  • Correcting a number that doesn't match its agreed definition
  • Updating a target figure you send us

FAQ

Questions owners ask first.

What do I need to send you?

Whatever you already use to run the business: a strategic plan, a scorecard, a spreadsheet, or a list of the numbers you check each week. Add your logo and the tools you use. We do the rest.

Is the first month refundable?

No. The first month pays for your wireframe and up to two rounds of changes, and that work happens during it. If the wireframe isn't right, cancel before approving it and you won't pay a setup fee.

What does the setup fee cover?

Defining your metrics, connecting your tools, reconciling the numbers against your records, building the approved design with your real data, and testing it before you go live. It is due once, when you approve your wireframe.

What counts as a change request?

Adding something new: a metric, a visual, a filter, a page section, a new tool, or a different calculation. Fixing anything that is wrong or broken, updating a target, and keeping the data refreshed are included and never count.

Who owns my data?

You do. We use it only to build and run your dashboard. If you leave, we can export your data and then delete it. See Data security.

Where is my data stored and who can see it?

In encrypted, access-controlled cloud databases in the United States. Only you, the people you invite, and the Verelia staff working on your dashboard can see it. Details are on our Data security page.

Do I need a data warehouse or a data team?

No. We host your data for you. Enterprise customers can have a dedicated Snowflake warehouse hosted by Verelia, or connect their own.

What if my tool isn't on your list?

Tell us when you sign up. If it has a way to export or share its data, we can usually connect it. We confirm every connection before you approve the wireframe.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Plans are month to month. The setup fee is not refunded once the build has started.
