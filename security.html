Data security

How we protect the data behind your dashboard

Your business data stays yours. We only access what your dashboard needs, we keep it separate from every other customer, and when you leave we export it for you and delete it.

Who has access

You, the people you invite, and the Verelia staff building or maintaining your dashboard. Nobody else. Staff access is limited to the work in front of them, and every login to the app is protected by Clerk.

How your tools are connected

  • You authorize each connection yourself, using the tool's own sign-in or a key you create, never by sharing your password with us.
  • We ask for read-only access wherever the tool offers it. We do not change records in your systems.
  • We only request the data needed for the metrics you approved in your wireframe.
  • You can revoke a connection at any time from inside the tool, or by asking us.

Where your data is stored

Customer data is stored in managed cloud databases in the United States, kept separate for each customer. Enterprise customers can have a dedicated Snowflake warehouse hosted by Verelia, or keep their data in their own warehouse and let us connect to it.

How it's protected

  • Encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest.
  • Payments handled by Stripe. We never see or store card numbers.
  • Credentials for your tools are stored encrypted and are never shown in the app.
  • Data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI models.

What happens if you leave

Cancel anytime. Within 30 days you can ask for an export of your dashboard data. After that we disconnect your tools and delete your business data. Billing records are kept only as long as tax rules require.

Reporting a problem

If you think something is wrong with the security of your data, email support@vereliaai.com with "Security" in the subject line and we will respond within one business day.
